Jay-S guide: what it is and how to use the app. Jay-S is a study app: you attach your own material (handouts, notes, past exams) and Jay-AI, an artificial intelligence model that runs on this computer, builds exams, mock tests and flashcards (study cards) from it. Your files never leave the computer. The side bar has the sections "Materials", "Exams", "Mock test", "Flashcards", "Jay-AI" and "Settings".

Jay-AI and what depends on it: the new questions of an exam, the cards of the flashcards, "Explain", the analysis of a result and the chat need Jay-AI to be downloaded (the "Jay-AI" section). Without it you can still attach materials and take the exams that are already built.

Attach material: how do I attach a PDF, a DOCX, a text file or a photo? In the "Materials" section, click "Attach files" and pick one or more files. Jay-S reads PDF, DOCX, text (.txt and .md) and photos (PNG and JPG), up to 200 MB per file. If the PDF has a password, save a copy without it and attach that one.

Attach by dragging: how do I attach files by dragging them? Drag the files onto the Jay-S window: three zones appear, "Content", "Exams" and "Answer keys", and the file becomes the kind of the zone you drop it on. Dropping outside the zones attaches nothing. You can also open a file with Jay-S from the Windows open with menu, and it is attached.

Kinds of material: what is content, an exam and an answer key? Each material sits in one of the tabs of "Materials": "Content" (handouts, notes, book chapters, photos of the board), "Exams" (past exams, such as ENEM, UEPG, UEM or your school's, to reuse and practice) and "Answer keys" (the answer keys of your exams). Jay-S tells which is which by itself, from the text; when it is not sure, the card shows "Check this". To change it, use the "What it is" list on the card: "Content", "Exam", "Content and exam" or "Answer key". A file attached in a tab becomes that tab's kind.

Material card: what can I do with an attached material? The card shows the pages, the letters and, for an exam, how many questions Jay-S found. "View" opens the material (the original and the extracted text), "Explain" asks Jay-AI for an explanation (it is not shown on answer keys) and "Delete" removes the material from Jay-S, while your own file stays where it is. Photos and scanned pages are read by the Windows text recognition, which needs a Windows language with text recognition.

Answer key of an exam: how do I attach the answer key? On the card of an exam there is the "Answer key" list, with "Automatic" (Jay-S looks for the attached key), "None: Jay-AI answers" and each attached key, and the button "Attach its answer key…". You can also attach it in the "Answer keys" tab, picking the exam in "For the exam". Without a key, Jay-AI answers the questions you reuse, and that is marked as "answer by Jay-AI".

Build an exam: how do I make a new exam? In the "Exams" section, click "New exam" and fill in the "Name", the "Style", the "How" ("New, from my content" or "Reuse exams"), the content for the new questions and "How many new questions (Jay-AI writes and checks each one)", from 0 to 50. Click "Build the exam"; "Stop" interrupts. The finished exam shows up in the list of Exams. New questions need Jay-AI downloaded and some content checked.

Exam styles: what are ENEM, UEPG and UEM? ENEM: five choices (A to E), one right, with a base text and then the command. UEPG (somatória) and UEM (somatória): propositions 01, 02, 04, 08 and 16, one to five of them true, and the answer is the sum of the true ones. In both, marking a false proposition makes the question worth zero; with no false one, you get the share of the true ones you marked.

Reuse exams: how do I use the questions of an exam of mine? In "New exam", pick "Reuse exams", check one or more attached exams and say how many of their questions to use. Each exam shows how many questions Jay-S found and whether there is an answer key. You can mix those questions with new questions from your content.

Ready-made exams: where do I find official exams that are already made? In the "Exams" section, "Ready-made exams" tab, pick the year and click "Download" on the exam you want. Jay-S downloads the file from the university's website, checks it against the original (SHA-256) and builds the exam with the university's official answer key; nothing of yours is sent and Jay-AI is not needed. "Stop" interrupts it and, when you download again, it continues where it stopped. When the exam shows "Ready", "Take the exam" opens it, and it also appears in "My exams". A question the university annulled shows "Annulled" and does not count in the score.

Take the exam: what is exam mode? In the list of Exams, "Take the exam" opens the exam in exam mode: one question at a time, with the question numbers on top to jump around, "Previous", "Next" and "Finish". Each answer is saved at once, you can change your mind, and the result only shows at the end. If questions are left blank, Jay-S asks before finishing.

Learning mode: what is learning mode for? In "Learning mode" (the button beside "Take the exam"), each question is graded at once: you mark the answer and click "Check" to see whether you got it right, the right answer and the explanation, when the question has one. Once checked, the question is locked. If you move to another question without clicking "Check", the marked answer counts anyway. It is for studying while you solve.

Leave an exam: what does the "Leave" button do? "Leave" goes back to the list without losing anything: the answers are kept and the exam shows up in "In progress", in Exams, with "Continue" (picks up where you stopped) and "Discard" (deletes the attempt and its answers, after asking). In a mock test the clock keeps running while you are away.

Mock test: how do I take a mock test? First build an exam in "Exams". Then, in the "Mock test" section, pick the "Exam", set the "Time (minutes)" and click "Start the mock test". The suggested time comes from the number of questions (about 3.5 minutes per question for ENEM and 4 for UEPG and UEM), and you can change it. A mock test is an exam in exam mode against the clock: the "Time left" shows on top and, when it runs out, the exam ends by itself with the answers given so far.

Result and analysis: where do I see the result of an exam or mock test? At the end, Jay-S shows the score, the "Jay-AI's analysis" (what went well, what to review and the next steps), the performance "By topic" and "Question by question", with what you marked and the right answer. The analysis needs Jay-AI downloaded and shows up a few moments later. Old results stay in "Results", in Exams and in Mock test, with "See the result" and the delete button.

Flashcards: how do I create study cards? In the "Flashcards" section, under "New deck", give a "Name", check the content, say "How many cards" (from 1 to 300) and click "Make cards with Jay-AI". It needs Jay-AI downloaded and content attached. The cards adapt: the topics you did badly on in exams come first, and the length of new cards follows your votes "Too long" and "Too short".

Flashcards from an exam: can I make flashcards (study cards) from an exam I took? Not directly: the cards are made only from content material ("Content" or "Content and exam"), never from an exam or a result. The result of your exams already helps the cards by itself, because the topics you did badly on come first in new decks. To go over what you got wrong, open the result and see "Question by question", or talk about the exam in the chat, under "An exam I took".

Review flashcards: how do I study the cards of a deck? Click "Review" on the deck. Click the card, or press Space or Enter, to flip it and see the answer, and pick how well you remembered: "Forgot", "Hard", "Good" or "Easy" (keys 1 to 4). It is spaced repetition: a card you forget comes back in 10 minutes and the others come back at longer and longer intervals. When no card is due today, the deck shows "nothing to review now".

Explain a material: what is Explain? In "Materials", the "Explain" button on the card of a content or an exam opens a screen where Jay-AI summarizes and explains the material in the language you choose, at one of three levels, "Very simple", "Balanced" or "In depth", with an optional focus in "Anything to focus on? (optional)". Click "Explain with Jay-AI". The result has the summary, the "Key points" and the "Concepts" with an example.

Explain questions: after the explanation, "Jay-AI asks you": it asks questions one at a time, you answer in your own words and send with the "Answer" button, and Jay-AI reads your answer and says "Right!", "Almost there." or "Not quite.", sometimes with a smaller question to help. There are "Show a hint" and "Next question". At the end, "Ask Jay-AI" lets you ask your own questions about that material.

Download Jay-AI: how do I download Jay-AI? In the "Jay-AI" section, "Model" tab, pick the model and click "Download". Jay-S downloads the engine (llama.cpp) and then the model, checks both against the original file (SHA-256) and deletes whatever does not match. The internet is used only for this download and for the ready-made exams you choose to download; "Stop" interrupts it and, when you download again, it resumes where it stopped. The button at the top of the window shows the state: "Jay-AI not installed", "Jay-AI off", "Jay-AI loading…", "Jay-AI ready" or "Jay-AI working…".

Standard or Lightweight model: which Jay-AI model should I pick? "Standard" is Qwen3.5 4B (about 2.7 GB): better questions, best with a graphics card or 16 GB of memory. "Lightweight" is Qwen3.5 2B (about 1.3 GB): for computers with little memory, with simpler questions. To switch, pick the other one and download it; "Remove model" deletes the downloaded model.

Turn Jay-AI on and off: do I need to turn Jay-AI on? There is no on button: it starts by itself when a feature needs it and leaves the memory 2 minutes after the last use, so otherwise it takes no memory. In the "Model" tab, "Free the memory now" turns it off at once and "Test Jay-AI" checks that it works.

What runs on the computer: does Jay-AI send my files to the internet? No. The model runs on this computer, in a llama.cpp process that only accepts connections from the computer itself and closes with Jay-S. The internet is used only to download the engine, the model and the ready-made exams you choose.

Jay-AI chat: how do I talk to Jay-AI? In the "Jay-AI" section, "Chat" tab, type in the "Where do we start?" field or click "New conversation". Below it, pick the subject: "Any question" (Jay-AI answers with what it knows and says there is no source), "My materials" (you check which; it answers from them, quoting the passages, and what they don't cover it answers from what it knows, labelled "Not in your sources") or "An exam I took" (an exam or mock test already finished; it sees the answer key, what you marked and the result). Enter sends, Shift+Enter makes a new line and "Stop" interrupts.

Chat badges: what do "From your sources", "Not confirmed" and the others mean? "From your sources": the sentences were confirmed in the materials, and the numbers beside them open the quoted passage. "Not confirmed": Jay-AI could not prove the sentence in the sources, and it appears dimmed and underlined. "General knowledge, no source": it answered with what it knows, with nothing to check it against. "Not in your sources": the chosen materials do not talk about this. "From the Jay-S guide": the answer came from this guide, about how to use the app.

How the chat checks answers: can I trust the answer? With materials or an exam, Jay-S picks the passages, Jay-AI answers copying from each passage the words that prove the sentence, and the program checks: the quote has to be in the passage, the numbers have to be in it or in the question, and the answer key and what you marked have to match the exam. Then Jay-AI makes a second reading. Below the answer are the sources with the quotes. Jay-AI can be wrong: read the quotes.

Settings: how do I change the theme, the language or the animations? In "Settings", "Appearance" has the "Style" ("Calm", "Neon" or "Soft"), the "Theme" ("System", "Light" or "Dark"), the "Animations" ("On" or "Off") and the "Language"; Jay-S reloads to change the language. The sun or moon button at the top of the window also switches between light and dark.

Limits of Jay-S: what does Jay-S not do or not manage to do? There are no open (written-answer) questions: the exams have only choices (ENEM) or sums (UEPG and UEM). A scanned exam, which is only an image, can lose the letters of its options, and then the answer key does not grade it right. Jay-AI can be wrong, mostly in calculations and formulas: check the quotes and the numbers.
